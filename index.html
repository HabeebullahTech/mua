About the Academy

Who We Are

Mirathul Ummah Academy is an online institution dedicated to structured education in Arabic Language and Islamic Sciences, including Qur'an, Hadith, Aqeedah, Fiqh and Islamic manners and character.

Background

Mirathul Ummah Academy is a division of Attobah Center for Islamic Propagation and Research, Ilorin, and is registered with the National Board of Arabic and Islamic Studies (NBAIS). The Academy was established to make structured Islamic education accessible to Muslims regardless of location or professional circumstances, through progressive academic levels and flexible online learning.

Philosophy

The Academy's approach combines sound Islamic creed, the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah, Arabic Language proficiency, Islamic manners and morals, structured academic progression, qualified instruction, practical understanding and flexible modern learning methods.

Knowledge → Understanding → Character → Action

Mission

To provide accessible, structured and quality Arabic and Islamic education through sound curricula, qualified teachers and flexible modern learning methods, while nurturing sound Islamic creed, knowledge, character and practical application.

Vision

To become a leading accessible institution for structured Arabic and Islamic education, nurturing knowledgeable, principled and confident Muslims capable of understanding their religion and contributing positively to society.

A Message from the Director

Dr. Yahya Abdul-Baqy Zakariyah

The pursuit of knowledge is among the most valuable journeys a Muslim can undertake. Our purpose at Mirathul Ummah Academy is to make structured Arabic and Islamic education accessible to those who sincerely desire to learn, regardless of where they live or what other responsibilities they carry.

Through a carefully structured curriculum, qualified teachers and flexible online learning, we hope to nurture students who possess not only knowledge, but also sound Islamic character and a sincere commitment to putting their knowledge into practice.

We ask Allah to bless this humble effort, make it beneficial and enable it to contribute to the preservation and transmission of beneficial knowledge for generations to come.
